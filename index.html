LaundryLogix

Streamlining Service Transactions Through A Digital Point of Sale System for Elyssa Laundry Center

LaundryLogix is a web-based digital POS system designed for Elyssa Laundry Center. It streamlines laundry transactions, machine management, inventory tracking, and customer communication through a connected mobile app. The system replaces manual, paper-based processes with an organized, efficient, and user-friendly platform that also supports analytics and real-time updates.

Key Features
